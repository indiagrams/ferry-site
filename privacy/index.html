Privacy

Ferry has no account and no server of its own. Nothing about you or what you share is collected or sent to indiagram.

What you share goes from your phone to the person you chose, encrypted on the way, and is not kept anywhere in between.

Ferry keeps a short record on your phone of what it did, so you can send a report if something goes wrong. You choose when to send it and who gets it. It says what Ferry did and when, with names taken out, and it never includes your photos or files.

Ferry has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. This page loads nothing from anywhere else.
